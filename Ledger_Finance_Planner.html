When this is on, each new after-tax paycheck uses the percentage sliders on your existing goals. If the sliders total more than 100%, automatic splitting pauses until you rebalance them.

Cash-flow baseline

Set the spendable money you had before your first Ledger transaction. This gives the running-balance and forecast tools a real starting point. Do not include money that was already reserved inside goals.
Until you add real accounts, Ledger treats this as one combined spendable balance. Extras remains spendable; active goal balances are treated as reserved.

Goals & Extras

Choose what Ledger does after a goal reaches exactly 100%. A goal balance itself is always capped at its target.
Any amount that would exceed a goal on the paycheck that completes it is always redirected to Extras. Default behavior also sends that completed goal's future percentage to Extras.

Security & recovery

A PIN can lock Ledger on open. Recovery mode can unlock it with either your recovery key or your saved security answer.
